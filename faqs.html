Getting started is simple and straightforward:

  1. Schedule an initial consultation through our contact form or by calling us
  2. We'll conduct a thorough assessment of your business needs
  3. Receive a customized proposal with recommendations

The entire process typically takes 2-3 weeks from initial contact to project kickoff.

We offer flexible pricing models to suit different business needs:

  • Fixed Project Fees: For well-defined projects with clear scope
  • Hourly Rates: For ongoing advisory and consulting work
  • Retainer Agreements: For continuous support and strategic guidance
  • Performance-Based Pricing: Tied to specific outcomes and KPIs

During our initial consultation, we'll recommend the most suitable pricing structure for your project.

We believe in complete transparency. Our proposals include:

  • Timeline and milestones

There are no hidden fees. Any changes to scope or additional work are discussed and approved in advance.
